Remote Work Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Remote work is normally permitted Monday through Friday, but teams may designate specific collaboration days when employees are expected to be onsite.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their team's core hours, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

Requests for fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.

The company may temporarily suspend remote work for business continuity, security, or regulatory reasons.
