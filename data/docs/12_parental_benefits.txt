Parental Benefits Policy

Effective date: March 15, 2026.

Eligible full-time employees may receive parental leave benefits in accordance with the company's parental leave program.

Employees should submit parental leave requests to HR as early as reasonably possible so that leave arrangements can be planned.

The standard parental leave benefit provides up to 12 weeks of paid leave for eligible employees.

Eligibility and benefit details may depend on the employee's employment status and applicable law.

Employees should contact HR for individual eligibility questions or documentation requirements.
