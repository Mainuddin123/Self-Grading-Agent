Performance Review Policy

Effective date: April 1, 2026.

Formal performance reviews occur twice each year, in June and December.

Managers are expected to provide written feedback covering outcomes, collaboration, and development goals.

Employees may add written comments to their review.

A review rating does not by itself guarantee a salary increase, promotion, or bonus.

Employees who disagree with factual information in a review should first discuss it with their manager and may then contact HR.
