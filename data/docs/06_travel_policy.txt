Travel Policy

Effective date: February 15, 2026.

Domestic travel requires manager approval before booking.

International business travel requires department-head approval and a completed travel request.

Hotel reimbursement is capped at INR 6,000 per night for domestic travel unless an exception is approved in advance.

Employees may select reasonable ground transportation.

Travel advances, when approved, must be reconciled within 15 calendar days after returning from the trip.
