Information Security Policy

Effective date: March 1, 2026.

Employees must use multi-factor authentication for company accounts where MFA is available.

Company credentials must never be shared with another person.

Sensitive company files must be stored only in approved company storage systems.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours of discovery.

Employees must not install unapproved software on managed company devices without IT approval.
