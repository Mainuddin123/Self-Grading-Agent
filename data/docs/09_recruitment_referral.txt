Employee Referral Policy

Effective date: February 5, 2026.

Employees may refer qualified candidates for open positions through the company's internal referral system.

A referral bonus of INR 10,000 is available when an eligible referred candidate is hired and completes 90 calendar days of employment.

Employees involved in the hiring decision for a referred candidate are not eligible for the referral bonus.

The referral bonus is paid through payroll after the eligibility requirements are confirmed.

Referrals must be submitted before the candidate enters the final interview stage.
