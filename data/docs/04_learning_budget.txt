Learning and Development Policy

Effective date: January 15, 2026.

Each full-time employee has an annual learning budget of INR 20,000 for approved courses, certifications, books, and conferences.

The employee's manager must approve the learning activity before registration or purchase.

Unused learning budget does not convert to cash and does not roll over to the next year.

New employees become eligible after completing 30 calendar days of employment.
