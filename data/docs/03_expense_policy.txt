Business Expense Policy

Effective date: February 1, 2026.

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses that are necessary for approved company work.

Receipts are required for individual expenses above INR 1,500.

Expense reports should normally be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.

Air travel in economy class is the standard for flights under 6 hours.

Business-class travel requires prior written approval from a department head.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
