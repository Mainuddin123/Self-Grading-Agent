Code of Conduct

Effective date: January 5, 2026.

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and business partners with respect and professionalism.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and retaliation are prohibited.

Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest to their manager or Compliance.

Company resources must be used primarily for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable company policies.

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

Employees who want to report a concern may contact their manager, HR, Compliance, or the confidential reporting channel.
