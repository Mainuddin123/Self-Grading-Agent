Leave Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026.

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Leave requests of five or more consecutive working days should be submitted at least 10 calendar days in advance when practical.

Sick leave may be taken when an employee is unwell; employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward for up to 5 days into the next calendar year.

Contractors are governed by their individual contracts rather than this policy.
