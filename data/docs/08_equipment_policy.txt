Company Equipment Policy

Effective date: January 20, 2026.

Eligible employees may request a company laptop for business purposes.

Standard company laptops are replaced on a four-year lifecycle unless the device is damaged beyond economical repair.

Employees must return company equipment when their employment ends.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

Employees are responsible for reasonable care of company equipment and must not intentionally modify hardware without IT approval.
