Data Retention Policy

Effective date: March 10, 2026.

Business records must be retained according to the company's applicable retention schedule.

Financial records must be retained for 7 years.

Employee personnel records must be retained for 7 years after the employee's separation date unless a longer period is required by law.

Routine system logs are retained for 90 days.

Records subject to an active legal hold must not be deleted or altered until the legal hold is formally released.

Employees should contact Legal or Compliance when they are unsure whether a record is subject to a retention requirement.
